BGEANX CFD Guide: how to get started carefully

A practical route to understand the platform, check applicable conditions and use risk tools with realistic expectations.

See the stepsOfficial BGEANX site
Key principle

Understand the product first, test the platform second, and only then decide whether a trade matches your risk tolerance. CFDs are leveraged and can generate rapid losses.

Six-step checklist

01

Identify the entity

Confirm the name, authorisation scope, country and documents shown during account opening.

02

Understand the CFD

You do not buy the underlying asset. Results depend on price movement, size, margin and costs.

03

Calculate costs

Review spread, overnight financing, conversion and third-party charges before an order.

04

Choose the order

Understand market and pending orders, take profit, stop loss and execution conditions.

05

Define margin

Leverage amplifies gains and losses. Set your maximum size and acceptable loss in advance.

06

Monitor positions

Follow margin, alerts, financing and news. Do not leave positions open without knowing their costs.

What to check

  • Products and leverage available in your region.
  • Instrument-level spreads and overnight financing time.
  • Margin requirements and forced-close rules.
  • Deposit and withdrawal methods, limits and processing times.
  • Support channels and complaints process.
Essential warning: negative-balance protection and segregated funds depend on entity and client type. They do not protect against market losses.
